# The Chilling Effects of Board Transparency — visual companion

A scrollytelling companion to the article *The Chilling Effects of Board
Transparency* by Roberto Tallarita (Harvard Law School).

Read the paper: https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7248478

## The site

`index.html` is the whole thing — a single self-contained file with no build
step, no dependencies, and no external assets. Open it in a browser, or serve
the directory:

    python -m http.server 8000

## How it reads

The argument runs straight through, but it branches twice. At the beat where
both effects are named, the two labels are a choice: clicking one opens that
effect's story (Elena's, for opposition-chilling; Sophia's, for sanitization),
and each story ends by offering the other. Scrolling past the fork instead goes
directly on to the taxonomy of records. A third side story — a survey of
Delaware litigators — is offered later, from the section on the statute, and
the closing beat has two click-to-explore panels on the doctrine.

Taking neither branch is about 38 beats; taking both is about 55.
